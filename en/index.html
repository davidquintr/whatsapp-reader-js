    How to export a WhatsApp chat

    1. Android: open the chat, tap ⋮ › More › Export chat and choose “Without media” (.txt) or “Include media” (.zip with photos and audio).
    2. iPhone: open the chat, tap the contact or group name › Export Chat, and save the file to Files.
    3. WhatsApp Web or Desktop: open the chat, click ⋮ › More › Export chat.

    Then open the file above with “Choose a file”, or drop it on the page.

    What you can do with WhatsApp Reader

    • Read the conversation with bubbles, dates and a color per participant, just like WhatsApp.
    • See photos, stickers, videos, voice notes and contacts if you exported with media (.zip).
    • Explore statistics: who writes more, when you talk, streaks and favorite emojis.
    • Search any message, filter by person and jump to a date.
    • Compare two exports to find new or deleted messages.
    • Save the chat as HTML or PDF, and install the app to use it offline.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my messages uploaded anywhere?

    No. The file is opened and processed only in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server, not even ours.

    Which formats are supported?

    The .txt and .zip files WhatsApp exports on Android, iPhone, WhatsApp Web and Desktop, in English or Spanish, including the 2024+ formats.

    Does it work with group chats?

    Yes. Each participant gets a color, and you can choose whose side of the conversation to see.

    Why can't I see the photos?

    If you exported “Without media”, WhatsApp only leaves a “media omitted” note. Export “Include media” and open the whole .zip.
